the stuff people ask.

is this an app?
no. it's a number. you text it like you text anyone. there's an optional imessage extension that adds the workout card, nothing to install otherwise.
how much does it cost?
free during the open beta. founding-member pricing comes later, and you'll hear about it from me first.
what data does cued use?
your texts, plus anything you choose to connect: calendar (next 60 days), canvas, google health. all read-only, all optional, all deletable with one text.
do i need a watch?
no. a watch makes the sleep and recovery stuff automatic. without one, just tell me how you slept.
who built this?
a berkeley student. see "made in berkeley" above.
